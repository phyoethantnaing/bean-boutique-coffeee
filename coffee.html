A simple starting point

Brew by weight, then trust your taste.

Begin with 20 grams of coffee and 320 grams of filtered water. Taste once the cup cools a little, then adjust one variable at a time.

  • If it tastes sour, grind a little finer.
  • If it tastes bitter or dry, grind a little coarser.
  • The best recipe is the one you enjoy.
